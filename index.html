FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about roof sheet installation, roof ventilators and gas cutting machines in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

Do you do roof sheet installation in Coimbatore and Kerala?

Yes. We install metal, polycarbonate and FRP roofing sheets for factories, warehouses and sheds in Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Erode, Salem and across Tamil Nadu, and in Kerala including Palakkad, Thrissur, Kochi and Kozhikode.

What is the turbo ventilator price in Coimbatore?

The price depends on the ventilator size, material and how many you need. Share your shed size and we will recommend the right number and size and send you a quote. Call or WhatsApp +91 96775 58464.

How many turbo ventilators does my shed need?

It depends on the roof area, the shed height and how much heat is produced inside. Send us your roof dimensions and what the shed is used for, and we will work out the number and placement for you.

Do turbo ventilators need electricity?

No. Turbo ventilators spin using wind and rising hot air, so there is no power cost. Where a shed needs stronger, forced airflow, we also supply electric ventilators (industrial exhaust fans).

Turbo, ridge or louver ventilator: which is right for my shed?

Turbo ventilators exhaust hot air at points on the roof. Ridge ventilators vent continuously along the roof ridge. Louver vents fit in the walls and let fresh air in. Many sheds work best with a combination, and we can suggest the right mix for yours.

Portable or CNC gas cutting machine: which should I buy?

A portable gas cutting machine runs on a track and makes straight, bevel and circle cuts, so it suits on-site work and smaller workshops. A CNC gas cutting machine is a gantry machine for oxy-fuel and plasma cutting of steel plates, ideal when you cut many parts or complex shapes.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Irugur, Coimbatore, and serve Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Erode, Salem, Namakkal, Karur, Pollachi and Mettupalayam in Tamil Nadu, and Palakkad, Thrissur, Kochi (Ernakulam), Malappuram and Kozhikode in Kerala.
